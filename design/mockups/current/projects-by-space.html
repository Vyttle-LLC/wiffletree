Today the sidebar shows every project in one list, whatever its Space. In this study each Space gets its own section, and the existing first-word groups sit inside it. 1 is today. 2 is the same nine projects listed by Space. 3 and 4 show dragging. New pieces have a dashed outline. All data is simulated.

Dropping in another Space does nothing. No insertion line appears there. Moving a project to another Space changes its repositories, so it stays in Overview › Move to another Space, which asks first.

What stays the same
  • The Group button still turns first-word groups on and off. Space sections are always shown, even with grouping off.
  • A new project lands at the top of its own Space.
  • Archived projects show inside their Space's section, as they do today.
  • Collapsing a Space, like collapsing a group, lasts until the app restarts. Selecting a project inside a collapsed Space opens it.
  • A Space with no projects to show has no section. The host stores no new data.
