Long decisions stay reachable

Today a long approval, such as a pull request with a full description, grows the decision card past the bottom of the window, so Deny and Open pull request cannot be reached. The fix caps the card's height and scrolls its middle, while the header and the buttons stay in view. Dashed outlines mark what changes.

Fix. The middle scrolls; the header, the buttons and the composer stay visible.

  • Cap. The card is at most about half the conversation pane's height. Short cards look exactly as they do now, with no scroll bar.
  • What scrolls. Everything between the card's header and its buttons: the prompt, the detail rows, the description, and a question's choices and answer box.
  • What stays put. The header row with the Approval or Question pill, the button row (Deny / Approve, Dismiss, Answer) and the composer below.
  • Every kind of decision. Questions, permission approvals and pull request approvals all behave alike. Switching decisions with the arrows starts the next one scrolled to the top.
  • Inbox panel. Unchanged; it already scrolls as a whole list.
