(b) Coordinator: one wake at cycle end, sent as Wiffletree rather than in the coordinator’s name, listing open blocking findings and the entries it still has to triage. The full ledger is on the ticket in workspace_context.

  • Accepted tickets archive their agents, so today they leave the tree unless Show archived is on. This study shows the archived view. Settled: waived tickets follow the same rule and show only with Show archived; the waiver is always on the Tickets panel card.
  • Implementers see only routed blocking findings, sent as Wiffletree. Verifiers see “already decided” entries (won’t fix, follow-up) in their round input and are told not to re-raise them unless the cited code changed.
  • This is a static design study, not the GPUI implementation.
