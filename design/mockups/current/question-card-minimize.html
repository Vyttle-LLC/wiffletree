Minimize a decision without dismissing it

The decision card above the composer gains a minimize control. Minimized, it shrinks to a single row that still says a decision is waiting, and the conversation gets its space back. Clicking the row, or its expand control, restores the full card. Dashed outlines mark what is new.

One row: the question’s first line, truncated, the position when there are several, and Expand. The yellow edge keeps it reading as “needs you”.

  • Restore: click anywhere on the row, or the Expand control. The full card returns showing the same decision.
  • Stays minimized while you read, type and send messages, and when you switch to another conversation and back. It resets to expanded when the app restarts.
  • A new decision arriving does not pop the card open; the row’s count updates instead (for example “1 of 3”).
  • When the last decision is settled the row disappears, as the card does today. The next decision that arrives starts expanded.
  • Nothing else changes: answering, Dismiss, Open inbox and the Inbox panel behave as they do now.
