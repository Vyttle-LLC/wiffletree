Design study · remove turn limits

Check-ins, and caps in plain words

Static study for the remove-turn-limits change, approved for the Settings window. Wiffletree has no turn limits. Instead, the coordinator hears about an agent that works many turns without it, and you hear about a coordinator that works many turns without you. Every control has a plain label and a one-line caption, including the Review card's two caps. Each choice saves at once. Values are simulated.
