Wiffletree · evaluator control overlay study

A stop button that stays on top

When a full-desktop evaluator runs, it brings its apps to the front, so getting back to the Wiffletree window to stop it may be hard. The helper therefore shows a small panel that floats above every window, on every Space and over full-screen apps, for the whole run.

Approved by Michael, 2026-11-23
  • Always visible: top-right corner, just under the menu bar, above every app, including full screen. You can drag it to another corner, and it remembers.
  • The evaluator can't touch it: it never appears in the evaluator's screenshots, and the evaluator's clicks on it are refused, like any app it wasn't given.
  • Three ways to stop, all equal: the panel's Stop button, Esc anywhere, and the menu bar item. Each ends the run at once.
  • Replaces the Wiffletree banner as the main control. The banner in the main window and the menu bar item stay, as backups.
