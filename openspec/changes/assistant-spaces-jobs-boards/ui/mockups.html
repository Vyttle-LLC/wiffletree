Spaces are made here, by the user. The assistant can suggest one but never creates it. Clone paths, allowed models and whether the Space is on stay on this Mac; the rest lives in space.yaml in the storage repository.

How it behaves

  • Threads are private and live only on this Mac. Idle threads leave the tab strip after 24 hours and stay searchable.
  • Threads read repositories, files, the web and their Space's brain, add log entries to that brain, and write only in their scratch folder. The provider enforces this.
  • The router never writes. Below the threshold, or when it fails, it asks.
  • Jobs are user-owned and separate from coordinator timers. Brain notes and other content cannot create one.
  • This study is static and awaits the human's approval. It is not the GPUI implementation.
