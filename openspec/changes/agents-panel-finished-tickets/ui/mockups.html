Only what still needs you stays in view

The Agents panel shows every accepted ticket at full size, so 32 finished tickets bury the 9 open ones. It also keeps a full row for every agent, busy or not. This study folds finished tickets, archives finished agents, quiets agents that are waiting, and leaves open work on top. Dashed outlines mark what is new. Tickets and agents are sample data modelled on MUDling.

How it behaves

  • Finished tickets means accepted and closed tickets together. The section is closed by default and sits above Previous agents.
  • A finished ticket's PR keeps being read until GitHub says it merged or closed, so its line is always true. This fix also corrects the five stale MUDling PRs on the first pass after the update, with nobody touching them.
  • An agent working, blocked, paused, disconnected or after a failed turn keeps its full row. Anything that needs a look is never quieted.
  • An agent waiting on something is active. The implementer stays on its ticket until the ticket is accepted or closed, on one quiet line that says what it waits for, and it goes back to a full row the moment it starts a turn.
  • An agent that is finished is archived. A verifier is archived as soon as it passes, and its row moves to Previous agents. A ticket's implementer is archived when the ticket is accepted or closed, as it is today.
  • The project's progress bar and percentage do not change.
