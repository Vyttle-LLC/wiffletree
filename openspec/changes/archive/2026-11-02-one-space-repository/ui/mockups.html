Approved by Michael, November 1, 2026

The change: a Space names one repository and one folder. Its issues, log entries and wiki all live there and are written the same way. Setting up a company takes one form.

Issues are what you call tickets. Log entries are what I called notes in our chat. Nothing here runs. Vyttle's repositories, folders and file counts are real. Issue counts, teammates, times and scan findings are sample data.

How it behaves

  • One way in. Issues, log entries and wiki changes are scanned on the Mac, then pushed straight to main inside the folder. A file that fails stays on that Mac.
  • Compile. Every Mac with compiling on is due at the set time. The first awake claims the run, and Git accepts only the first claim. A claim from a crashed Mac expires after an hour. A Mac out of model quota skips, and the next one takes the run.
  • Nothing to compile. No claim, no commit and no model call.
  • Conflicts. The same Inbox cards as today. A ruling is written straight in, and the next compile picks it up.
  • No Mac compiles. Logs keep landing, and the Brains and Space pages say the wiki is falling behind.

What leaves the app

  • The separate brain and storage settings, and the rule that a repository can't be both.
  • Batch branches, auto-merge, watching brain pull requests, and redact or discard on a batch.
  • Set up, Update gate, the required GitHub checks and CODEOWNERS lines for a brain.
  • The compiler named in wiffletree-brain.json, and the warnings for the old second-brain skills.
