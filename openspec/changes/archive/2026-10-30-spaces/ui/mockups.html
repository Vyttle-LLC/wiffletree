Approved by Michael, October 29, 2026

A static study for openspec/changes/spaces. It copies the approved Space settings page from assistant-spaces-jobs-boards and adds three things: the Space's storage repository is also its tracker, New project starts in a Space with that Space's repositories, and the Repositories page is grouped by Space. Nothing here runs: no repository is cloned, pushed or set up. Sage, Vyttle and Personal are sample Spaces; names, times and figures are drawn by hand.

A repository may be in several Spaces, with one chip each, or in none. Only you change membership; agents cannot.

How it behaves

  • A Space's storage repository holds its definition, its jobs and boards, and its issues. Several Spaces may share one storage repository, and then share its issues and prefix.
  • Wiffletree keeps one clone and one sync per GitHub repository, under repos/ in its data folder. Each folder in it keeps its own rules: settings and Spaces pause edits made outside Wiffletree; issues merge field by field and accept them.
  • The brain stays a separate repository with its own pull-request flow.
  • Repositories stay a record on this Mac (clone path, base, checks). A Space lists them by owner/name only, and that list is what both the Space page and the Repositories page edit.
