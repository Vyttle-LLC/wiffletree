How it decides something is a repeat

  1. Wiffletree, not the model, detects repeats. When the coordinator replies to a turn you didn't start, Wiffletree compares the workspace's recorded facts with the moment of its last shown update: ticket states, commits on each ticket branch, review cycles and findings, PRs, questions and stopped agents. If any of them changed, the reply stays full and opens with a Since last update line listing the changes. If none changed, the reply folds as No change.
  2. Nothing is lost. A folded reply keeps its full text one click away, as agent messages do today. Folding is display only. If the coordinator wrote something important in a no-change turn, it can mark the reply as news so it stays full.
  3. The ticket table stops repeating. A ticket board pinned at the top of the chat shows each ticket's status and PR from the workspace's records, so it's always current. The coordinator's instructions drop the table from routine updates and keep it for when a ticket is finished, accepted, published or ready for you.
  4. Always full: your messages, the coordinator's replies to them, failures and blocked reports. Decisions that need you still go to the inbox card.
