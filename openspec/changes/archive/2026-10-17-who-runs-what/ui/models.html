When a ticket's PR is open

Watch it
Always on. Tells the coordinator when it merges, closes, fails checks or conflicts.
Keep it rebased
After the base has been quiet for 2 minutes. Pushes only when the change is the same as the reviewed one.
Try once to fix a conflict or failed checks
Then runs the Light review before pushing.
Sort new comments
Tells the coordinator which need a change or an answer.
