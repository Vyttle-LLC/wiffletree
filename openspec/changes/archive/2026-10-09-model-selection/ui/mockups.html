(a) Models page, per machine. Four cards. Providers is the hard limit: which subscriptions this machine has and which models and efforts may run. Roles picks providers for coordinators, implementer and tester. Review sets the standard reviews (one per ticked provider, in parallel after the tester) and free-text instructions for custom reviews. Guide is prose on how to pick the model within a provider. The ticks below are today's real policies migrated (the union of allowed lists).

Guide how coordinators pick the model and effort within a provider

Choose the model and effort within the role's provider by the size and risk of the work. Claude: small, contained change → sonnet; large or cross-cutting → opus. Codex: routine review → gpt-6.1-sol · medium; large or risky change → gpt-6.1-sol · high. Raise effort for migrations and security-sensitive code. ## Sizing (migrated 2026-10-08) Coordinators · Claude: large work → opus · high; small work → opus · medium. Implementer · Claude: large work → opus · medium; small work → sonnet · high. Tester · Claude: large work → opus · high; small work → sonnet · high. Reviewer · Claude: large work → opus · high; small work → sonnet · medium. Reviewer · Codex: large work → gpt-6.1-sol · high; small work → gpt-6.1-sol · medium. Maintenance was fixed to gpt-6.1-sol · medium (tiers: low → gpt-6.1-sol · low, complex → gpt-6-astra · high).
