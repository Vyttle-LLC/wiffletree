Six slices, each its own PR of about 300 production lines or less (tests and Markdown not counted), in this order. Each leaves the later ones out and is useful alone. Every requirement of the delta specs belongs to exactly one slice, the one that completes it, named at the head of that slice; an earlier slice may build part of it, and its tasks say which scenarios they verify. A slice's PR implements its tasks and syncs its own requirements, and only those, into the main specs. Line numbers are at skills slice 2's commit ee931652, rechecked when slice 1 started. Slice 1 starts after skills slice 2 merges, and slice 4 after skills slice 5 (decided question 8). No slice reads or writes the human's own settings, `Sage-Social/wiffletree-storage` or any real repository: tests use temporary data folders and local bare repositories, with `gh` answered by a fake as in `publish.rs`'s tests.
